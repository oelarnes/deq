DEq
DEq, or Estimated Draft Equity, is a card quality metric designed to be used as a pick order in booster draft. The value represents the expected win rate added to your deck by selecting the given card versus a basic land. The metric adjusts GP WR by the opportunity cost as recorded in the ATA statistic, in addition to other minor adjustments, and modifies the result by GP% to account for differences in play rate. For more details, see the docs. Data is sourced daily from 17Lands. DEq is not affiliated with 17Lands.
